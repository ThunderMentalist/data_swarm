# Stakeholder Stage Spec

## Purpose
Map roles, stance, decision rights, and engagement paths.

## Inputs
- triage brief
- planner output
- stakeholder KB subset + policy

## Outputs
- `03_stakeholders/03_stakeholders.json`
- critic / curator / change artifacts

## Approval semantics
- Manual operator approval required.

## Sub-agents
Concierge: map roles.
Critic: validate governance/completeness.
Curator: summarize durable learnings.
Change: proposal/history only.
